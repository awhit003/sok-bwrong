# Inspirations & Design Vision

**Project:** *s'ok-b'wrong*  
**Platform:** Panic Playdate  

---

## 1. The Core Vision (In Creator's Own Words)

> "Baba only in spirit. Mainly, AAAAXY (only in concept) but the crank is Braid (but not like Braid at all - more like Crankin's Time Travel Adventure but Zelda style), and the whole feel is like P.T. and looks like Undertale but plays like Moonlighter and 12 Minutes for some reason I don't recall, and I don't know where the idea with duck that is actually a key but is actually a boss fight with tentacles and you have to die or you will not progress... where do these inspirations come from??! I have played thousands of games."

> "I spent thousands of hours between 1998 and 2009 playing roughly anything a bored gate guard with a handheld gaming fetish would have played. I was a soldier, and the AAFES PX was just a few doors down. They had, over time, the Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, and even a Nintendo DS that fit perfectly in the standard NATO MOLLE size pouch. In the Iraqi desert, I was gunning, normally patched-through Sony Discman through the ICOM, blasting the soundtrack to *O Brother, Where Art Thou?* and *Dizzy Up the Girl* to my crewmates’ pleasure. It was to my crewmates’ displeasure that this was sometimes replaced by the chiptunes from *Pokémon Gold* (I did not want to play the game on duty, but needed to advance to the next track by a number of button presses; I explained to my superiors when questioned).
> 
> One of my favorite games at the time of enlistment was *Desert Strike*."

## 2. Inspiration Breakdown

* **Hardware / Screen Feel**: 2D tile map on a 1.8" to 2.7" sharp pixelated unbacklit/reflective LCD. Direct sunlight readability.
* **Desert Strike**: Tactical tension, logistics, mission checklists, navigating hostile sectors.
* **AAAAXY (concept)**: Non-Euclidean geometry and spatial looping.
* **Crankin's / Braid (Zelda style)**: Top-down movement on D-Pad, crank scrubs room timeline.
* **P.T.**: Looping corridors that subtly decay and mutate.
* **Undertale**: 1-bit aesthetic, quirky facade masking darker undertones.
* **Moonlighter & 12 Minutes**: Time-loop iteration, gathering knowledge/items across cycles.
* **The Duck/Key/Tentacle Boss**: Absurd item utility turned into cosmic horror; mandatory death to progress.
